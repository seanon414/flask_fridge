    What does coast do?

    After the compressor stops, the freezer keeps cooling for a while, so the temperature slides below the low edge of your range. Coast is how many minutes that slide lasts. The controller watches how fast the temperature is falling and stops early, so it settles near the low edge instead of well under it. A bigger number stops earlier; 0 stops exactly at the low edge.

    Trade-off: shorter runs and more cycles per day. It never stops more than 2°F early and still keeps the 5-minute minimum run.
